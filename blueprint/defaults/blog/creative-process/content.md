## Finding Your Process

Every creative person I admire has a different process. Some sketch obsessively before touching a screen. Others dive straight into the medium and let the work reveal itself. There's no right answer — only what works for you, right now.

## What Works for Me

I've settled into a rhythm that looks something like this:

- **Collect** — I save everything that catches my eye. Screenshots, photos, bookmarks, scraps of text. No filter, no judgment.
- **Connect** — Every few weeks I sit with my collection and look for threads. What keeps showing up? What am I drawn to?
- **Create** — Once a direction feels clear, I work fast. The first pass is never precious. Speed keeps the inner critic quiet.
- **Refine** — This is where the real work happens. Editing, iterating, asking hard questions about whether the piece actually says what I want it to.

## The Uncomfortable Part

The gap between *having an idea* and *making the thing* is where most projects die. I've learned to treat that discomfort as a signal, not a stop sign. If it feels hard, it usually means the idea matters.
